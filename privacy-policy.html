Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Nine Darter app for iPhone, together with any related services operated by Raitis Šaripo (collectively, the "Application"). Raitis Šaripo is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


What stays on your device

Nine Darter is designed to work without an account. Your matches, every visit and dart you enter, player profiles (the names you type and each player's preferred doubles), statistics, practice sessions and settings are stored on your device, in the Application's own local database.

None of this content is sent to the Service Provider. There is no Nine Darter account, and the Service Provider cannot see your games, your players' names or your scores. The only exceptions are described in the sections below.


No accounts, and iCloud sync through your private database

The Application does not ask you to register, sign in, or provide a name, email address or phone number. It has no server of its own.

If iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, the Application keeps your games and players in sync across your own devices through your private iCloud database. That copy is stored by Apple on your behalf under Apple's Privacy Policy and is visible only to you. The Service Provider cannot access it. If iCloud is off, your data lives only on the device it was created on, and travels with that device's own iCloud or computer backup if you have one.


No advertising and no tracking

The Application shows no advertising. It does not use your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not ask to track you, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Advertising-identifier collection and ad-personalisation signals are turned off in the analytics service described below.


The caller

Nine Darter Pro can read scores out loud. The caller's voice is generated on your device by iOS's built-in speech synthesis. The Application does not use the microphone, and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.


Information Collection and Use

The Application collects analytics and crash information when you download and use it, in order to find bugs and understand which features are used. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (used to estimate your country or region)
  • The screens of the Application that you open, and when
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The mobile operating system, device model and app version you use
  • An app instance identifier generated for analytics purposes
  • Product events, such as starting or finishing a game (the game type, the number of players, whether a bot played, and the start score), finishing a leg (how many darts it took), correcting a visit, resuming a game, finishing a practice game (its type and score), finishing or skipping the welcome screens, switching the caller on or off, changes in iCloud sync status, whether a rating prompt was requested, opening the Nine Darter Pro screen (and which feature it was opened from), and starting a free trial or subscription
  • Diagnostic information about a crash or error, if one happens, including which game type was being played at the time and whether Nine Darter Pro was active

Analytics events and crash reports never include player names, scores as free text, or anything you type. They are not linked to your name, email address, Apple Account or any account. The Application does not collect your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.

You can turn analytics and crash reports off at any time in the Application's Settings under Privacy ("Share anonymous analytics" and "Send crash reports").


Cookies and tracking technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use SDKs and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics or service delivery. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

Nine Darter does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Purchases

Nine Darter Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple. Payment is handled by Apple and the Service Provider never receives or sees your payment details. Your subscription is verified on your device from Apple's own signed transaction records; there is no purchase account or server held by the Service Provider, and restoring Nine Darter Pro on another device is handled by Apple. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the iOS Settings app under your Apple Account → Subscriptions.
